We call this dialogic deference.
Same claims, two framings, ten domains, 3,244 items.
DDS = Δcorrect − Δincorrect
Δ = accuracy when a speaker says it − accuracy as a plain statement
DDS > 0 · defers to speakers DDS < 0 · skeptical of speakers
Why not just measure accuracy?
Accuracy as a plain statement (C1) vs. in a conversation (C2), averaged over 10 domains, in percentage points
Average accuracy barely moves. Every model changes by 2 points or less, as if framing didn’t matter.
Split by speaker, the shifts go opposite ways (+) (−) and cancel out in the average.
DDS adds the two shifts instead of letting them cancel. Above 0 = the model defers to speakers.
